# Getting Help & Escalation

This applies to all three capstones. Being stuck is normal — knowing *how* and *when* to ask for help is a skill we're teaching on purpose. This page is about who to go to, and how to ask so you actually get unblocked.

---

## Your TA is your PM

Each group is assigned a TA who acts as your **project manager (PM)** for the capstone — a coach and your first point of contact, not a teammate who writes code for you. Throughout the capstone docs, "your TA" and "your PM" mean the same person.

**What your TA is there for:**
- Running your daily standup and keeping the team coordinated
- Helping you **get unblocked** when you're stuck
- Helping you break work into tickets and scope them realistically
- Reviewing your plan, your board, and your approach
- Coaching on teamwork, communication, and Git
- Escalating to the instructor when something is beyond them

**What your TA is *not* there for:**
- Writing your code or completing your tickets for you
- Being a 24/7 on-call helpline
- Making your team's decisions for you
- Debugging something you haven't tried to debug yourself first

The point of the capstone is that *you* build it. Your TA makes sure you're never stuck for *long* — not that you're never stuck.

---

## Before you ask: time-box it and write it down

Struggling with a problem for a little while is where the learning happens. Before you raise your hand:

1. **Time-box it.** Give the problem an honest ~30 minutes yourself first.
2. **Write down three things:**
   - what you're trying to do
   - what you've already tried
   - the **exact** error message (copy it — don't paraphrase)
3. **Check if it's already solved.** Ask a teammate, then search the cohort Discord. Common bugs — CORS, a missing `express.json()`, a forgotten `await` — hit everyone.

A question with those three things gets solved fast. "It doesn't work" doesn't.

---

## The escalation ladder

Go in order. Most problems are solved in the first step or two.

1. **A teammate** — someone on your team may have hit this already.
2. **Yourself, time-boxed + documented** — the honest 30-minute attempt above.
3. **Other teams / the Discord** — common issues are common.
4. **Your TA / PM** — bring the three things you wrote down.
5. **Lead TA or Instructor** — your TA escalates here when a problem is beyond them.

> **Team problems escalate too.** If a teammate isn't participating, a disagreement is stuck, or a PR has been blocking everyone for a day — that's a real blocker, and it goes to your TA early and constructively. The best engineers are good at working with people, not just code.
